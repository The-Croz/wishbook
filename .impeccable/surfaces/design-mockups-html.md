# Wishbook app screens (phone PWA)

Mode: Operate. Audience: family members on phones, couch, evening indoor light; includes grandparents. Job: browse a person's list, claim gifts, add gifts by pasting links, manage own/kid lists. Also: logo options.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your list is a page in the family Wish Book: wants are circled in marker, claims get a check. Refuses the white-card wishlist app with a red plus button.
OWN-WORLD: Committed red (#C8102E) cover shell, white newsprint pages, ink #1A1A1A, mustard #F2B705 for highlights, pine #2E7D5B only for claim marks. Archivo Black display, Atkinson Hyperlegible body, hand-drawn SVG marker loops and checks. Item numbers and big catalog prices.
STORY: Giver sees who has claimed what and picks something unclaimed; owner sees only their wants, never claims; adding is paste a link and confirm.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Red header with person name and counts, a two-column catalog grid of gifts with photo, item no + store, title, price; marker loop on "most wanted", check + "Josh has it" on claimed (giver view only); bottom tab bar; primary action at thumb reach.
FORM: Wish Book, position 1 on my ordered list (pick card). seed:635077efa33b61a0
SIGNATURE: the marker loop/check drawn on with a stroke animation when you mark most-wanted or claim.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
